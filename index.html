GridHabit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 April 2026

GridHabit (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a habit-tracking app for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. We have written it in plain English. If anything here is unclear, email support@sedhuops.cloud.

1. We do not require an account

You do not sign up, log in, or give us your name or email to use GridHabit. All your habits, check-ins, notes, reminders, and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We do not copy this data to our servers. We do not have servers that store your habit data.

2. What stays on your device

  • The habits you create and their colour tags.
  • The check-ins you record (complete, partial, incomplete) for each day, week, and month.
  • Any notes you write.
  • Your reminder times and quiet-hours settings.
  • Your theme choice (light or dark).

You can export this data to yourself (Settings → Export data) or erase all of it permanently (Settings → Danger Zone → Erase all data). Uninstalling the app also removes everything on your device.

3. Information that leaves your device

GridHabit shows ads and offers an optional one-time premium upgrade. To make those features work, a small amount of information is sent to two trusted third parties. We do not read or receive this data ourselves — it goes directly from your device to the third party.

3a. Google AdMob (ads)

AdMob (a Google service) shows banner ads on some screens for users who have not purchased the premium upgrade. AdMob collects:

  • Your Android advertising ID (a resettable ID you control in system settings).
  • Device information: model, operating system version, language, country, IP address.
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address — not GPS).
  • Coarse app-usage information needed to serve and measure ads.

AdMob uses this to serve ads and prevent ad fraud. We request a "PG"-rated ad content filter so adult creatives are never shown. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a Google-provided consent form the first time you open it, and AdMob honours your choices.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3b. RevenueCat (purchases)

If you tap "Remove ads" and buy the premium upgrade, the purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. We use RevenueCat to remember that you paid so the upgrade works across devices signed into the same Google account. RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymous, app-generated identifier (not your name or email).
  • The product you purchased and the purchase receipt from Google Play.
  • The country and platform the purchase was made on.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

3c. Google Play Billing

When you buy the premium upgrade, the transaction is processed by Google Play. Google Play receives your payment details — we never see your card or UPI information. Google's Play-specific privacy terms apply.

3d. Local notifications

Habit reminders are scheduled and fired on your device by your operating system. Reminder content is not sent over the network.

4. What we do NOT collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts.
  • No precise location (no GPS).
  • No microphone, camera, photo-library, or file access.
  • No health, fitness, or biometric data.
  • No third-party analytics SDK (no Firebase Analytics, no Mixpanel, etc.).
  • No cross-app tracking.

5. Your controls

  • Reset your advertising ID — Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Opt out of personalised ads — same Android Settings page, or via the in-app consent form (EEA / UK / Switzerland).
  • Disable notifications — Android Settings → Apps → GridHabit → Notifications, or from inside the app.
  • Export your data — Settings → Export data (gives you a JSON file you own).
  • Erase all your data — Settings → Danger Zone → Erase all data. This permanently deletes every habit, log, note, reminder, and setting on the device.
  • Uninstall — removes everything the app has stored locally.

6. Children

GridHabit is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you want us to help remove any data that ended up with AdMob or RevenueCat, email support@sedhuops.cloud.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because habit data never leaves your device, we have nothing to delete from our servers — we have no such servers. AdMob and RevenueCat retain the data described above according to their own retention policies (linked above), and honour user-initiated deletion requests sent through their controls.

8. International users

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, California, or another jurisdiction with a data-protection law, you have the right to know what data is handled about you, request deletion, and object to processing. Because we hold no server-side data, most of these rights are already exercisable via the in-app controls in section 5. For anything else, email support@sedhuops.cloud and we will help you contact AdMob or RevenueCat directly.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change what data the app sends or adds a new third-party service, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will also be announced inside the app before taking effect.

10. Contact

Questions, requests, or feedback: support@sedhuops.cloud.
